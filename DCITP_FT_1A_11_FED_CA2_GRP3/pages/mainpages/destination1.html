About

About Marina Bay

Marina Bay is a vibrant waterfront district in the southern part of Singapore that serves as one of the country's premier destinations for tourism, business, entertainment, and recreation. Known for its iconic skyline, the area is home to famous landmarks such as Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, the Merlion, and the Singapore Flyer. Marina Bay offers luxury hotels, shopping malls, fine dining, cultural attractions, and scenic waterfront promenades, making it a popular destination for both locals and international visitors. It also hosts major events such as the Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix and the annual New Year's Eve countdown celebrations.

History

Marina Bay was created through extensive land reclamation works carried out by the Singapore government in the 1970s and 1980s, expanding the country's limited land area. The bay was officially formed in 1971 when the mouths of the Singapore and Kallang Rivers were dammed and reclaimed to support Singapore's long-term urban development plans. Over the following decades, the area transformed from reclaimed land into a modern mixed-use district with world-class infrastructure, commercial developments, residential buildings, and tourist attractions. Landmark projects such as Marina Bay Sands, which opened in 2010, further established Marina Bay as a symbol of Singapore's economic growth, innovation, and global city status.

Hotel

Overview

Marina Bay Sands is one of Singapore's most iconic luxury hotels and integrated resorts. Officially opened in 2010, it has become a global landmark due to its unique architecture, world-class hospitality, and famous rooftop Infinity Pool. Located in the heart of Marina Bay, the resort combines luxury accommodation with shopping, entertainment, fine dining, a casino, museums, and convention facilities, making it one of the country's most visited tourist destinations.

Famous Attractions Within the Hotel

Infinity Pool

Location: Hotel Tower 3, Level 57, Marina Bay Sands

Opening Hours: 6:00 AM - 12:00 AM

Price: Exclusive to Hotel Guests

The Infinity Pool is Marina Bay Sands' most famous attraction and the world's largest rooftop infinity pool, located on Level 57. Exclusive to hotel guests, it offers breathtaking panoramic views of Singapore's skyline and Marina Bay. It is one of the most photographed swimming pools in the world.

SkyPark Observation Deck

Location: Hotel Tower 3, Level 57, Marina Bay Sands

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM (Last admission around 9:30 PM)

Price: S$35 - Adults, S$31 - Children

Located on Level 57, the SkyPark Observation Deck is open to the public and provides spectacular 360-degree views of Singapore's skyline. Visitors can see famous landmarks such as Gardens by the Bay, the Singapore Flyer, and the Central Business District. It is a popular spot for sightseeing, photography, and watching the sunset.

The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands

Location: 10 Bayfront Avenue, Basement 1 to Level 2

Opening Hours: 10:30 AM - 11:00 PM

Price: Free

The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands is a luxury shopping mall featuring over 170 international brands, designer boutiques, restaurants, and cafés. Visitors can also enjoy the unique indoor canal, where traditional sampan boat rides are available. It is one of Singapore's premier destinations for luxury shopping and dining.

Helix Bridge

Details

Location: Linking Marina Centre and Marina South, Singapore

Opening Hours: Open 24 hours

Rating: 4.7⭐

Price: Free

About

The Helix Bridge is a unique pedestrian bridge inspired by the structure of human DNA. It connects Marina Centre to Marina South and offers excellent photo opportunities, especially at night when the bridge is illuminated.

Singapore Flyer

Details

Location: Singapore Flyer

Address: 30 Raffles Avenue, Singapore 039803

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM

Rating: 4.6⭐

Price: 40SGD - Adults, 25SGD - Children

About

Standing 165 metres tall, the Singapore Flyer is one of the world's largest observation wheels. Visitors can enjoy breathtaking 360-degree views of Marina Bay, the city skyline, and even parts of Malaysia and Indonesia on clear days.

Recommended MRT Station

For most visitors, Bayfront MRT Station is the most convenient option because it is directly connected to Marina Bay Sands, The Shoppes, ArtScience Museum, and Gardens by the Bay via underground walkways. It is served by both the Circle Line and Downtown Line, making it easy to reach from most parts of Singapore.

Recommended Bus Stop

The Marina Bay Sands Theatre and Opp Marina Bay Sands Theatre bus stops are the closest to the hotel and shopping mall. Numerous SBS Transit and SMRT bus services stop here, providing convenient access to nearby attractions such as Gardens by the Bay, the Singapore Flyer, and the Central Business District.
