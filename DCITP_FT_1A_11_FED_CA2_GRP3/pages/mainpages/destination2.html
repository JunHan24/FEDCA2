About

About

Gardens by the Bay is an award-winning nature park spanning 101 hectares in the heart of Singapore's Marina Bay district. Opened in 2012, it is one of Singapore's most popular attractions, featuring over 1.5 million plants from around the world. The gardens are best known for their iconic Supertree Grove, the Cloud Forest, and the Flower Dome, which showcase diverse plant species in beautifully designed environments. Combining nature, sustainability, and innovative architecture, Gardens by the Bay provides visitors with a unique experience while supporting Singapore's vision of becoming a "City in Nature."

History

The idea for Gardens by the Bay was announced in 2005 as part of Singapore's plan to transform Marina Bay into a vibrant garden city and strengthen its reputation as a global destination. Construction began in 2007 on reclaimed land beside Marina Reservoir, and the gardens officially opened to the public on 29 June 2012. Since then, it has become one of Singapore's most recognisable landmarks, attracting millions of visitors every year. Today, Gardens by the Bay is internationally recognised for its sustainable design, environmental conservation efforts, and iconic attractions, including the towering Supertrees and climate-controlled conservatories.

Attractions

Cloud Forest

Details

Location: Gardens by the Bay

Address: 18 Marina Gardens Drive, Singapore 018953

Opening Hours: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM

Rating: 4.8⭐

Price: S$32 - Adults, S$18 - Children (combined ticket with Cloud Forest)

About

Cloud Forest is a spectacular indoor conservatory featuring a cool mountain environment and one of the world's tallest indoor waterfalls. Visitors can explore elevated walkways through lush tropical vegetation while learning about biodiversity and environmental conservation. It is one of Singapore's most iconic nature attractions.

Flower Dome

Details

Location: Gardens by the Bay

Address: 18 Marina Gardens Drive, Singapore 018953

Opening Hours: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM

Rating: 4.7⭐

Price: S$32 - Adults, S$18 - Children (combined ticket with Cloud Forest)

About

The Flower Dome is the world's largest glass greenhouse and showcases thousands of plants from Mediterranean and semi-arid regions around the world. Seasonal floral displays are updated throughout the year, providing visitors with a different experience during each visit. Its beautiful landscapes make it a favourite destination for photography.

Supertree Observatory

Details

Location: Supertree Grove

Address: 18 Marina Gardens Drive, Singapore 018953

Opening Hours: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM

Rating: 4.5⭐

Price: S$14 - Adults, S$10 - Children

About

The Supertree Observatory is located at the top of the tallest Supertree, offering panoramic views of Gardens by the Bay and Singapore's skyline. Visitors can enjoy breathtaking scenery from the open-air observation deck while learning about the innovative design and sustainability features of the Supertrees.

Recommended MRT Station

The Gardens by the Bay MRT Station is the most convenient station, as it is directly connected to the gardens via an underground link. It provides the quickest access to attractions such as the Flower Dome, Cloud Forest, and Supertree Grove.

Recommended Bus Stop

The Gardens by the Bay bus stop on Marina Gardens Drive is the closest stop to the main entrance, just a 2-minute walk away. It is the most convenient option for visitors travelling by bus and provides easy access to the conservatories and Supertree Grove.
