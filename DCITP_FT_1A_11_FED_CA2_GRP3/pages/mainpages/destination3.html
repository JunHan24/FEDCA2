About Orchard Road

Orchard Road is known as the shopping paradise of Singapore, offering a wide range of retail experiences from international luxury brands to local fashion stores. Popular shopping malls along Orchard Road include ION Orchard, Ngee Ann City, 313@somerset, Orchard Central, and Plaza Singapura.

History of Orchard Road

Orchard Road was originally an agricultural area with fruit orchards, pepper farms, and nutmeg plantations in the 19th century. The area developed into a commercial district in the 20th century, with the opening of Plaza Singapura in 1974 beginning its transformation into Singapore's leading shopping destination.

Overview

Orchard Road is Singapore's most famous shopping and lifestyle destination. Located in the central area of Singapore, it is a 2.4-kilometre-long boulevard filled with shopping malls, luxury boutiques, restaurants, hotels, and entertainment attractions. It attracts both locals and tourists who come to experience Singapore's vibrant retail and urban lifestyle.

Famous Attractions Within the Orchard Road

ION Orchard
Details

Location: 2 Orchard Turn, Singapore 238801

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM Daily

Rating: 4.5⭐

Price: Free

ION Orchard is one of Singapore's most iconic shopping malls, featuring luxury brands, international fashion retailers, restaurants, and observation spaces. Its distinctive futuristic architecture has become a landmark of Orchard Road.

Ngee Ann City
Details

Location: 391A Orchard Road, Singapore 238872

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM – 9:30 PM Daily

Rating: 4.4⭐

Price: Free

Ngee Ann City is one of Singapore's largest shopping complexes and is home to Takashimaya Department Store. Visitors can enjoy luxury shopping, dining, bookstores, and entertainment facilities within the complex.

313@somerset
Details

Location: 313 Orchard Rd, Singapore 238895

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM

Rating: 4.4⭐

Price: Free

313@somerset is one of Orchard Road's most popular shopping malls, located directly above Somerset MRT Station. Opened in 2009, the mall was developed by Lendlease and has become a key lifestyle destination for both locals and tourists. It features over 140 retail, dining, and lifestyle outlets across eight levels, offering a mix of international fashion brands, restaurants, cafés, and entertainment options.

About

FLNT is a contemporary Nikkei restaurant and bar located on Level 55 of ION Orchard in Singapore.
