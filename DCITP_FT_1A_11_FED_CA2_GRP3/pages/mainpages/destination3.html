About and History of Orchard Road

About Orchard Road

Orchard Road is known as the shopping paradise of Singapore, offering a wide range of retail experiences from international luxury brands to local fashion stores. Popular shopping malls along Orchard Road include ION Orchard, Ngee Ann City, 313@somerset, Orchard Central, and Plaza Singapura.

History of Orchard Road

Orchard Road was originally an agricultural area with fruit orchards, pepper farms, and nutmeg plantations in the 19th century. The area developed into a commercial district in the 20th century, with the opening of Plaza Singapura in 1974 beginning its transformation into Singapore's leading shopping destination.

Overview

Orchard Road is Singapore's most famous shopping and lifestyle destination. Located in the central area of Singapore, it is a 2.4-kilometre-long boulevard filled with shopping malls, luxury boutiques, restaurants, hotels, and entertainment attractions. It attracts both locals and tourists who come to experience Singapore's vibrant retail and urban lifestyle.

About

Old's Uncle Ice Cream Stand by Walls is one of Orchard Road's most famous street food stalls. Visitors can enjoy traditional ice cream sandwiches served with colourful rainbow bread or crispy wafers. This nostalgic snack has become a beloved part of Singapore's food culture and is popular among both locals and tourists.

About

The Istana is the official residence and office of the President of Singapore. Built in 1869, it is one of Singapore's most important historical landmarks. The Istana features beautiful gardens, elegant architecture, and rich heritage. Although it is usually closed to the public, visitors can explore the grounds during selected Istana Open House events held on public holidays.
