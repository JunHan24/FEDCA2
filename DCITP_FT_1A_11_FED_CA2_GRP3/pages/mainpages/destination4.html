About National Gallery Singapore

National Gallery Singapore is a world-class art museum located in Singapore's Civic District. Opened in 2015, it is housed in the historic former City Hall and Supreme Court buildings. The gallery showcases the world's largest public collection of Singaporean and Southeast Asian modern art, preserving the region's artistic heritage while promoting cultural understanding through exhibitions and educational programmes.

History

The gallery's buildings played important roles in Singapore's history. City Hall witnessed major events such as the Japanese surrender after World War II and the swearing-in of Singapore's first Cabinet. The former Supreme Court served as the nation's highest court before both buildings were converted into the National Gallery. The gallery officially opened in 2015 after extensive restoration works.

Former Supreme Court Terrace

Details

Location: Level 4M, Former Supreme Court Wing

Address: 1 St Andrew's Rd, Singapore 178957

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM

Rating: 4.6⭐

Price: Free

About

The former Supreme Court has two domes, including the smaller Rotunda dome, which is now visible up close after the addition of a new floor. The Supreme Court Terrace is now repurposed as an events venue and public gathering space that offers a stunning architectural experience. In the day, the terrace is bathed in soft patterns of light and shadow, as the filigree canopy above filters light like a delicate veil across the historic buildings.
