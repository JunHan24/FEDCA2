About National Gallery Singapore

National Gallery Singapore is a world-class art museum located in Singapore's Civic District. Opened in 2015, it is housed in the historic former City Hall and Supreme Court buildings. The gallery showcases the world's largest public collection of Singaporean and Southeast Asian modern art, preserving the region's artistic heritage while promoting cultural understanding through exhibitions and educational programmes.

History

The gallery's buildings played important roles in Singapore's history. City Hall witnessed major events such as the Japanese surrender after World War II and the swearing-in of Singapore's first Cabinet. The former Supreme Court served as the nation's highest court before both buildings were converted into the National Gallery. The gallery officially opened in 2015 after extensive restoration works.

Famous Attractions Within the National Gallery Singapore

Former Supreme Court Dome

Details

Location: National Gallery Singapore, Level 6

Opening Hours: Daily, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (Last Admission:6:30 PM)

Visitors typically spend 3 hours here.

Rating: 4.7⭐

Price: Included with Gallery Admission

The Former Supreme Court Dome is one of the most iconic features of National Gallery Singapore. Visitors can explore the historic dome and enjoy panoramic views of Singapore's Civic District, Marina Bay, and surrounding landmarks while learning about the building's rich history.

Singapore Gallery

Details

Location: National Gallery Singapore

Opening Hours: Daily, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Price: Included with Gallery Admission

The Singapore Gallery showcases the nation's artistic development from the 19th century to the present day. It features works by renowned Singaporean artists and provides insight into Singapore's culture, history, and identity through art.

UOB Southeast Asia Gallery

Details

Location: National Gallery Singapore

Opening Hours:Temporaily closed (Reopen in late 2027)

Price: Included with Gallery Admission

The UOB Southeast Asia Gallery features one of the region's largest collections of modern and contemporary Southeast Asian art. Visitors can discover artworks from countries such as Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines.
