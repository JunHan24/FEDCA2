About Sentosa

Sentosa is a resort island located off the southern coast of Singapore, home to an array of themed attractions, beaches, resort hotels, and championship golf courses. Its name means "peace and tranquillity" in Malay, and today it is one of Singapore's most popular tourist destinations, accessible via the Sentosa Express monorail, cable car, boardwalk, or bus from HarbourFront on the mainland. Major draws on the island include Resorts World Sentosa, home to Universal Studios Singapore and Singapore's first casino, alongside beaches, spa retreats, and a yachting marina at Sentosa Cove.

History

The island was formerly known as Pulau Blakang Mati and served as a British military base during World War II. In 1970, the Singapore government renamed it Sentosa as part of a plan to transform it into a leisure destination, and the Sentosa Development Corporation was established in 1972 to oversee its development, backed by a S$124 million investment plan. A cable car system connecting the island to Mount Faber on the mainland opened in 1974, and further milestones followed over the decades, including the launch of Sentosa Cove in 2003 and the opening of Resorts World Sentosa in 2010.

Hotel

Overview

One of the most family-friendly resorts on Sentosa is Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa, designed with the aim of keeping kids happily occupied while parents unwind. The dedicated kids' club, Cool Zone is a real highlight and kids are often reluctant to leave. Guests enjoy a beachfront pool, board games, ping pong and free bikes to keep the whole family entertained, as well as a genuinely warm and attentive style of service - from birthday surprises left in the room, to staff who go out of their way to make celebrations memorable.

Famous Attractions Within the Hotel

Siloso Beach

Location: 10A Siloso Bch Walk

Opening Hours: Opens 24 hours

Price: Free

Siloso Beach is a lively stretch of white sand on Sentosa Island's western coast, known for its beach bars, water sports, and buzzing weekend atmosphere. It's also home to Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa, Singapore's only beachfront resort, making it a popular base for both relaxation and activity.

Nestopia

Location: Hotel Tower 3, Level 57, Marina Bay Sands

Opening Hours: Closed (Mon & Tue), 2:00PM - 6:45PM (Wed-Fri), 10AM - 7PM (Sat & Sun)

Price: 12SGD (Adults & Children)

Nestopia is a whimsical, nature-themed play area at Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa designed for younger guests, featuring interactive elements inspired by birds' nests and the surrounding tropical greenery. It offers a safe, imaginative space for kids to play and explore right within the resort grounds.

Adventure Cove Waterpark

Location: 14 Sentosa Gateway, Sentosa Island

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Price: 40SGD - Adults, 30SGD - Children

A family-friendly water park on Sentosa with slides, a lazy river, and a rainbow reef where kids can snorkel among tropical fish, making it an easy, fun day trip just a short ride from the hotel.
