About

About Jewel Changi Airport

Jewel Changi Airport is a nature-themed lifestyle and retail complex located at the heart of Singapore's Changi Airport, directly linked to Terminal 1 and connected via bridges to Terminals 2 and 3. Designed by architect Moshe Safdie, the multi-storey glass-and-steel dome is best known for the HSBC Rain Vortex, the world's tallest indoor waterfall, surrounded by the lush Shiseido Forest Valley. Beyond its centrepiece attractions, Jewel houses over 280 retail and dining outlets, a rooftop Canopy Park, and the YOTELAIR transit hotel, making it a destination in its own right rather than just an airport facility.

History

Jewel was built over the site of Changi Airport's former open-air Terminal 1 car park, with construction beginning in December 2014 as part of Changi Airport Group's long-term plans to expand the airport's capacity and reinforce its status as a major aviation hub. The S$1.7 billion project opened to the public on 17 April 2019. An official opening ceremony was held on 18 October 2019. Since then, Jewel has become one of Singapore's most visited attractions, drawing both transit passengers and local residents with its combination of nature, retail, and entertainment under a single glass dome.

Attractions & Facilities

Overview

Jewel Changi Airport is open 24 hours a day, though most individual shops, restaurants, and paid attractions follow standard operating hours of roughly 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. The complex is free to enter, with the HSBC Rain Vortex and Shiseido Forest Valley open to all visitors at no cost; only Canopy Park and a handful of adventure attractions on Level 5 require paid admission.

Famous Attractions Within Jewel

HSBC Rain Vortex

Location: Ground level, centre of Jewel Changi Airport

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM (Mon-Thu), 10:00 AM - 11:00 PM (Fri-Sun & Public Holidays)

Price: Free

Cascading 40 metres from the oculus at the top of the dome down to the basement, the HSBC Rain Vortex is the world's tallest indoor waterfall and the centrepiece of Jewel. After dark, it transforms into a free Light & Sound show held hourly through the evening, extending later on Fridays, weekends, and public holidays.

Canopy Park

Location: Level 5, Jewel Changi Airport

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM (Mon-Thu), 10:00 AM - 11:00 PM (Fri-Sun & Public Holidays)

Price: From approximately S$8 for general admission

Sitting atop the dome, Canopy Park is a rooftop recreational garden featuring the Topiary Walk, Petal Garden, Foggy Bowls, and Discovery Slides. Separately ticketed attractions such as the Mastercard Canopy Bridge, Hedge Maze, Mirror Maze, and the Manulife Sky Nets are also located here.

Shiseido Forest Valley

Location: Levels 1-2, surrounding the HSBC Rain Vortex

Opening Hours: Open 24 hours

Price: Free

Wrapped around the base of the Rain Vortex, the Shiseido Forest Valley is a terraced indoor forest planted with more than 2,000 trees and palms and over 100,000 shrubs, offering walking trails through greenery rarely found inside an airport.

Shopping & Paid Attractions

Hedge Maze & Mirror Maze

Singapore's largest hedge maze challenges visitors to find their way to a viewing point at its centre, while the neighbouring Mirror Maze offers a playful, optical-illusion filled walk-through for families and photo opportunities.

Pokémon Center Singapore

Details

Location: #04-201/202/203 Jewel Changi Airport

Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM, Daily

Rating: 4.1⭐

About

Located at Jewel Changi Airport, Pokémon Center Singapore is the first permanent Pokémon Center in Asia outside Japan. It offers a wide selection of official Pokémon merchandise, including plush toys, trading cards, video games, and Singapore-exclusive items, making it a popular attraction for both locals and tourists.

Getting There

Jewel is directly connected to the Arrival Hall of Terminal 1, with link bridges to Terminals 2 and 3, and a free shuttle bus service from Terminal 4. The Changi Airport MRT Station, on the Changi Airport Branch Line of the East-West Line, is connected to Jewel via Changi Airport Terminal 2 and is a short walk away.
