About Singapore Botanic Gardens

The Singapore Botanic Gardens is a tropical garden established in 1859 spanning Tanglin and Bukit Timah, and Singapore's first UNESCO World Heritage Site. Free to enter and open from early morning to midnight, the Gardens are home to heritage trees, lakes, themed gardens, and the world-renowned National Orchid Garden. A favourite spot for walking, running, picnics, and outdoor concerts, it remains one of the most visited and best-loved green spaces in Singapore.

History

The Gardens were founded in 1859 by the Agri-Horticultural Society on the site of a former plantation. Under the direction of botanist Henry Ridley from 1888, the Gardens played a pivotal role in developing rubber cultivation techniques that helped establish Southeast Asia's rubber industry in the early 20th century. The Gardens' orchid breeding programme, which began in 1928, grew into one of the world's largest orchid collections and led to the opening of the National Orchid Garden in 1995. In 2015, the Singapore Botanic Gardens became Singapore's first UNESCO World Heritage Site and the only tropical garden on the list.

Gardens & Attractions

Overview

The main Gardens are open daily from 5:00 AM to midnight and are free to enter. Within the 82 hectare grounds, the National Orchid Garden is the only section that charges admission, while themed gardens, lakes, and heritage trails are open to all visitors at no cost.

Famous Attractions Within the Gardens

National Orchid Garden

Location: 1 Cluny Road, Singapore Botanic Gardens

Opening Hours: 8:30 AM - 7:00 PM, Daily (last entry 6:00 PM)

Price: S$15 - Adults, discounts for seniors and students

Rating: 4.8⭐

Home to over 1,000 orchid species and 2,000 hybrids, the National Orchid Garden is the crown jewel of the Gardens. Highlights include the VIP Orchid Garden, where hybrids are named after world leaders and celebrities, and the Tropical Montane Orchidetum, a cool-climate glasshouse added in 2021.

Jacob Ballas Children's Garden

Location: 1H Cluny Road, Singapore Botanic Gardens

Opening Hours: 8:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Tue-Sun (closed Mondays)

Price: Free (adults must be accompanied by a child aged 14 or below)

Rating: 4.5⭐

Asia's largest children's garden, featuring a water play area, treehouse playground, and a working farm and orchard. Entry is restricted to children and their accompanying adults, keeping the space safe and family-focused.

Swan Lake

Location: Singapore Botanic Gardens, near Tanglin Gate

Opening Hours: Swan Lake is accessible during the Singapore Botanic Gardens' opening hours (5:00 AM - 12:00 AM daily)

Price: Free

Rating: 4.7⭐

The oldest and largest lake in the Gardens, Swan Lake is ringed by heritage trees and a swan sculpture at its centre, making it a popular spot for quiet walks and photography.

Other Features

  • Multiple heritage and nature trails, including the Rainforest Trail
  • Symphony Lake, host to open-air concerts
  • Six visitor centres with amenities and wheelchair loan services
  • Direct access to Botanic Gardens MRT Station
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site status since 2015
